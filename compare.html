Comparisons

Data and summaries · Comparisons · How to use · Methods · Downloads · Sources

Run these comparisons yourself in R.

Selected products are compared after unit conversion on the coarser product grid. Agreement is not accuracy. Methods documents the calculations and limitations.

National maps show sampled means in 100 km hexagons with at least five paired observations; peat maps use 40 km hexagons with at least three. A and B share a colour scale within each figure. Red differences mean A is higher, blue mean B is higher; extreme colours saturate (2nd–98th percentile for A/B; 95th percentile of absolute differences). Grey means insufficient paired data.
