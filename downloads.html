Downloads

Catalogue tables, comparison data and how to reproduce this site.

These are catalogue metadata, not individual measurements: study and data links, asset IDs, scales, depths, methods and status. Keep identifiers as strings; blank numeric cells mean unresolved or not applicable, never zero.

Comparison data

  • Comparison statistics: every pair, with n, means, mean difference, RMSD, Pearson r, Spearman ρ and the share within ±25%.
  • Pair samples: the comparison-grid values at each random point (pair_samples_*.csv), the profile interval values and map values at profiles.
  • Pair definitions: quantities, units, conversions, grids and depth datum for each pair.

Raster and observation access

Exact Earth Engine IDs and original-provider links are in the Assets table. Earth Engine sign-in and your own project are required even for public assets. Full-size rasters and ground tables should come from the original provider or be exported from the documented public asset in your own environment.

Reproduce

Rendering this site uses only committed files: Quarto and the R packages listed in Comparison/README.md, with no Earth Engine access. Refreshing the analysis needs Earth Engine and the original prepared profile tables; the steps and settings are in the same README.

Carbon State of Data for Canada. No map is treated as truth; agreement is not accuracy.
