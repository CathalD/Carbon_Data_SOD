Carbon data for Canada

Explore ground observations, environmental covariates and maps for Canadian forests and soils. See what the data contain, how they compare, and where to access them. GEE tools for using the data are planned.

Coverage is strongest for forest biomass and terrestrial soils; regional coastal, wetland and other resources are included where documented. This is not an exhaustive inventory of every Canadian carbon dataset.

What the comparisons show (details)

  • McMaster/WWF forest carbon vs SCANFI: McMaster/WWF (above + below + dead) is a median 1.57× SCANFI aboveground carbon, and the two share their spatial pattern (r = 0.59). The pools differ, so they are not expected to match.
  • Sothe (McMaster/WWF) vs SoilGrids, 0–100 cm: the two soil stock maps are close to uncorrelated (r = 0.07), with the largest gap in the peat-rich Hudson Plain (median 124 vs 22 kg C/m²), where SoilGrids excludes overlying organic layers.
  • Peat thickness: Li et al. and PEATGRIDS agree within ±25% at 67% of sampled Hudson Plain points, but their whole-column carbon does not.
