What we hold on forest and soil carbon, and how far it is verified

A descriptive inventory of the catalogue: field observations, the covariates studies used, and the carbon maps built from them. Counts, coverage, scale and depth support are reported as recorded. Nothing here harmonises units or depths, compares map values, or removes duplicate profiles.

Soil carbon

Field data

Profile and layer observations we have prepared and hosted, read from the local tables.

Counts are not independent. WoSIS, the National Pedon Database and the peat compilations share profiles; reconcile them before counting observations or building validation folds. Layers within a core are repeated depth observations, not separate locations.

Where the profiles are

One panel per dataset, other datasets in grey for context. Coordinates rounded to 0.01° for display.

How this page was built

Generated by Comparison/state_of_data_summary/build_summary.py and gee_map_previews.py. Sources: the catalogue workbook (Studies, Ground_data, Study_ground, Covariates, Maps, Other_sources), the repo QC register and depth audit, the local prepared profile and layer CSVs, and live Earth Engine reads.

Descriptive only. No unit conversion, depth harmonisation, stock integration, map differencing, duplicate removal or modelling was performed. Map previews are orientation aids, not analysis products.
