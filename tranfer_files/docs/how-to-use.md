# How to use the data

Google Earth Engine apps are planned. This page will provide tools to explore ground data, environmental covariates and maps. The sections below are placeholders for those apps; existing access examples are linked separately.

## Forests

### Ground data

**App forthcoming.** Explore forest plots and sampled-tree resources, their measurements, dates and spatial support. Some records require a request to the provider; retain coordinate precision and access terms.

### Covariates

**App forthcoming.** Explore environmental source variables, their coverage, units, dates and documented roles in forest studies.

### Maps

**App forthcoming.** View forest biomass and carbon products and summarise a chosen area. Keep biomass and carbon distinct and state the pools, reference year and valid mapped area.

## Soils

### Ground data

**App forthcoming.** Explore profiles and cores, depth coverage, SOC, bulk density and prepared site stocks. Keep the depth datum, measured/estimated status and source exclusions visible.

### Covariates

**App forthcoming.** Explore environmental source variables and their documented roles in soil and peat mapping studies.

### Maps

**App forthcoming.** View soil and peat products and summarise a chosen area. Keep concentration, density, fixed-depth stock, peat thickness and full-column peat carbon separate.

## Tool draft available for testing

The [Carbon Data Explorer script](../tools/gee/carbon_data_explorer.js) is a draft for running in the Earth Engine Code Editor. It includes AOI-clipped maps/covariates, on-demand soil profiles and raster/CSV exports. See its [instructions](../tools/gee/README.md). It has not been published as a company GEE app; the app placeholders remain the intended public tools.

## Access available now

- [Catalogue downloads](downloads.md) link original sources and exact assets where recorded.
- [Working Code Editor and Python examples](../examples/README.md) demonstrate data access. They are examples rather than the planned GEE apps.
- [Public access notes](public_access.md) describe checks performed in the source project.
- [Methods](methods.md) describe units, depth, pools and comparison eligibility.

Use an Earth Engine-enabled project that you control. Provider terms and dataset access requirements still apply. A site stock in kg C/m² is a stock per unit area; an area total also requires the mapped area and its coverage.
